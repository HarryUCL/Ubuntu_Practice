1.
chown改变所有者
-R 递归操作
将software的所有者从root改成hamster
hamster@hamster-virtual-machine:/opt$ sudo chown hamster software
hamster@hamster-virtual-machine:/opt$ ll
drwxr-xr-x  2 hamster root 4096 11月 23 18:54 software/

2.
改变所属组chgrp
hamster@hamster-virtual-machine:/opt$ sudo chgrp xitian software
hamster@hamster-virtual-machine:/opt$ ll
drwxr-xr-x  2 hamster xitian 4096 11月 23 19:02 software/
